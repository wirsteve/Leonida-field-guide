Scope: things to try in free roam for a player in mid Chapter 2 (Port Gellhorn / Kelly County; Vice City reachable by road). This pass skips everything already covered in pass 1 and the trophy/100% exclusions.

## Items to try (24)

### 1. Roadside cryptid in the swamp
- **Description:** On a quiet dirt road in the swamps near the Keys, keep an eye out for something big and hairy having a roadside meal, then follow it into the woods.
- **Surprise:** He bolts into the trees. Deeper in, there's a creepy human effigy wrapped in green mist. Breathe it in and you black out, Bigfoot comes over to inspect you, and you wake up by the lakeside as if he carried you there.
- **How to do it:**
  1. Drive the road south of Hamlet near Cottonmouth Lake, roughly across the water from Key Lento.
  2. Look for him crouched over a deer carcass at a dirt-road intersection.
  3. Chase him on foot into the swamp until you reach the effigy.
  4. Stand in the mist until you pass out.
- **What you need:** Nothing. Any character.
- **Where:** Swamp road south of Hamlet / Cottonmouth Lake (Leonida Keys side).
- **Risk:** None reported. You can't kill him at this first sighting.
- **Requirements:** Available after the very first mission. He's also part of a later Strangers & Freaks questline. Don't push players into that; the roadside sighting stands on its own.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Sources:**
  - Game8, "All Easter Eggs and Secrets" (read): https://game8.co/games/GTA-6/archives/638651
  - GamesRadar+, Bigfoot sightings (read; NZ early-unlock window): https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-bigfoot-sightings-are-already-ramping-up-as-rockstar-honors-a-22-year-old-grand-theft-auto-legend/

### 2. Yard-sale treasure hunt in Port Gellhorn
- **Description:** Follow the balloon-tied "Yard Sale" signs in southern Port Gellhorn and dig through a neighbor's driveway junk for a $5 hat with a history.
- **Surprise:** It's Arthur Morgan's cowboy hat from Red Dead Redemption 2, sold as a "Genuine Cowboy Hat".
- **How to do it:**
  1. Drive to the southern tip of Port Gellhorn (Kelly County, west coast).
  2. Find the small cluster of about five houses northwest of the Dirt Merchants mod shop, and follow the yard-sale signs.
  3. Look for the cluttered driveway with balloons, a big US flag and a cowboy cut-out. The hat sits on a guitar case on a couch at the back.
  4. Buy it and wear it.
- **What you need:** $5.
- **Where:** Southern Port Gellhorn. That's the player's current area.
- **Risk:** None.
- **Requirements:** None reported.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Sources:**
  - PC Gamer (read): https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/arthur-morgan-hat-location-rdr2/
  - Game8 (read): https://game8.co/games/GTA-6/archives/638651

### 3. The conspiracy garage
- **Description:** Track down a modest cream bungalow with two front-yard palms in Belville (it was teased in a 2021 GTA remaster painting) and peek inside its garage in daylight.
- **Surprise:** The garage wall is a huge red-string mind map with "IT'S A SIMULATION" in the middle. The NPC who lives there has worked out he's in a video game, and there are boat brochures lying around for his escape plan.
- **How to do it:**
  1. Drive around Belville (Leonard County) looking for the bungalow with two palm trees out front.
  2. Go during the day.
  3. Enter the garage and look at the wall and the scattered brochures.
- **What you need:** Daytime. IGN reportedly describes having to break into the garage.
- **Where:** Belville. The exact street isn't published, so point players to the neighborhood only.
- **Risk:** Breaking in may count as trespassing and get you a wanted level. Bank your cash first.
- **Requirements:** None reported.
- **Confidence:** high (one reputable read plus two independent reports in snippets)
- **Sources:**

### 5. Movie-car garage heist
- **Description:** Break into a villa garage on Vespucci Island and drive off with a very famous orange-and-green-era tuner.
- **Surprise:** It's Brian O'Conner's 1995 Mitsubishi Eclipse from The Fast and the Furious, and it needs no upgrades.
- **How to do it:**
  1. Drive to Vespucci Island in the Rialto Islands.
  2. Find the villa garage and break in.
  3. Drive it away.
  4. To keep it, register it at a Pay 'N' Spray.
- **What you need:** Break-in tools/skills. A free storage slot if you plan to register it.
- **Where:** Vespucci Island, Rialto Islands (Vice City area).
- **Risk:** Breaking in can draw police. Pocket cash is lost if you're busted or wasted.
- **Requirements:** Registering stolen cars unlocks during Chapter 2 (PowerPyx), so a mid-Chapter-2 player may not have it yet. They can still joyride it.
- **Confidence:** high

### 7. The fast-food bulk challenge
- **Description:** Try to make your character as heavy as the game allows by eating junk food and skipping the gym.
- **Surprise:** Players who hit the cap ("True Fat Jason", "max weight Lucia") found the heaviest build is surprisingly modest, nowhere near Arthur Morgan's heaviest.
- **How to do it:**
  1. Eat at least four burgers or tacos from fast-food chains.
  2. Sleep.
  3. Eat more fast food.
  4. Avoid all workouts. The player who did it said a loading screen, such as a vehicle repair, seemed to make the change appear.
- **What you need:** Cash for food.
- **Where:** Any fast-food spot.
- **Risk:** Stat effects aren't documented. PowerPyx warns that sleeping repeatedly without proper eating or exercise hurts your stats, so try it on a spare save.
- **Requirements:** None.
- **Confidence:** medium (GamesRadar+ read, citing X posts)
- **Sources:**
  - GamesRadar+ (read): https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-players-confirm-true-fat-jason-and-max-weight-lucia-arent-very-fat-at-all-setting-the-stage-for-the-first-pc-mod-to-super-size-them/

### 11. Incognito getaway
- **Description:** Commit a small crime in disguise, then try to slip past a patrol car.
- **Surprise:** Covering your face makes you harder to recognize, and you can slump down below the dashboard. Players report driving right past cops this way.
- **How to do it:**
  1. Buy a mask (Chuck's Tackle & Shot or most Ammu-Nations) and set its wear style to cover your face. A bandana, glasses or hat from the item wheel also helps.
  2. After a crime, get in a car.
  3. Hold Circle/B to duck low as police search the area.
- **What you need:** A mask or accessories. A car.
- **Where:** Any town.
- **Risk:** If spotted, your wanted level stays or rises. Pocket cash is lost if busted.
- **Requirements:** None.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Sources:**
  - PC Gamer, hidden controls (read): https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/15-hidden-gta-6-controls-and-features-you-should-know-about/

### 12. Raid the cop truck
- **Description:** Find a police pickup truck parked without its officers and see what's in the crate in the bed.
- **Surprise:** Pickups have no alarm. You can hop in the back and take shotguns, rifles, ammo and sometimes a stun gun. (Regular cruisers' trunks are alarmed.)
- **How to do it:**
  1. Find an unattended police pickup (Bravado Bison), for example near the Port Gellhorn police station.
  2. Climb onto the bed.
  3. Open the storage crate and grab the loot.
  4. Stow the long guns in your own trunk so you're less conspicuous.
- **What you need:** An empty police pickup.
- **Where:** Rural roads, or police stations in Port Gellhorn and Vice City.
- **Risk:** If cops see you, expect a wanted level. Cruisers need the slim jim from the Chapter 2 "Moses" mission to avoid the alarm.
- **Requirements:** None for pickups.
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Sources:**
  - PC Gamer (read): https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-free-guns-cop-cars/

### 13. Rail-line escape
- **Description:** When the helicopters come for you, leave the roads and use the railway as your escape route.
- **Surprise:** Police cars rarely spawn along wilderness tracks. Players also report that shooting at a police helicopter while you drive can make it break off the chase.
- **How to do it:**
  1. With a high wanted level, get on a fast bike or car.
  2. Head for the train tracks: the south-west of Vice City, or the north of Ambrosia, which has a tunnel.
  3. Follow the tracks out of the search area.
  4. Optionally shoot at the chopper.
- **What you need:** A fast vehicle, ideally a sports bike.
- **Where:** Rail lines in south-west Vice City or north Ambrosia.
- **Risk:** High-wanted chaos. Bank your cash first.
- **Requirements:** None.
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Sources:**
  - PowerPyx tips (read): https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-tips-tricks

### 16. Coast-to-coast in a seaplane
- **Description:** Borrow a seaplane and time yourself flying from the southern tip of the Keys to the northern edge of the map.
- **Surprise:** None. PC Gamer's run took about 4 min 18 s, a little under an hour of in-game time, and it reveals a strip of the map along the way.
- **How to do it:**
  1. Take the Sunbelt Highway south of the Keys starting house to the nearest unattended planes.
  2. Take a Mammoth Dodo seaplane.
  3. Fly north in a straight line and time it.
  4. Land on water if you can.
- **What you need:** A seaplane. Buy a parachute first if you might bail.
- **Where:** Planes off the Sunbelt Highway (Keys). Any plane works if you start elsewhere.
- **Risk:** Do not fly over the military base; PC Gamer got a 4-star wanted level. Jumping out without a parachute kills you and costs your pocket cash. Fuel is needed.
- **Requirements:** None.
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Sources:**
  - PC Gamer (read): https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-how-long-to-fly-across-map/

### 24. Holster and chill
- **Description:** In a tense standoff with police, put your weapon away instead of shooting and see how the officers respond.
- **Surprise:** A player clip shared on launch day suggests that putting your guns away can calm the situation down. Guides also describe a hands-up surrender prompt when officers close in.
- **How to do it:**
  1. When police confront you, holster your weapon. Long guns can't be concealed, so stow them in your trunk.
  2. Don't make sudden moves.
  3. Watch whether they de-escalate.
  4. Optionally, hold R1/RB at the surrender prompt.
- **What you need:** Nothing.
- **Where:** Any street.
- **Risk:** Surrendering counts as busted, so you lose pocket cash. Bank your money before trying it.
- **Requirements:** None.
- **Confidence:** low (TechRadar live-blog snippet; surrender steps only from small guide sites)
- **Sources:**
  - TechRadar launch live blog (snippet): https://www.techradar.com/live/news/gta-6-launch-day-november-19
  - TheClick.gg surrender guide (snippet): https://www.theclick.gg/gta-6-how-to-surrender-to-police/
