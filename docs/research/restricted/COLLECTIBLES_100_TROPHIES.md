## Headline facts

- **55 trophies**: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. On Xbox: 55 achievements, 1,000G, with the Platinum replaced by a 0G "Mythic" achievement (B1). The tier split appears in P1, G1, B1 and R1 (**high**). The Gamerscore values in B1 add up to 1,000, and R1 gives the same values.
- All trophies are offline and single-player. One playthrough is enough. There are no difficulty settings, no New Game+ and no mission replay, but free roam continues after the story. Cheats disable trophies and saving (P1, B1, S1). Cheats only unlock after the story by meeting conditions (S6).
- **28 hidden** trophies (B1 states 28; R1's italics mark the same 28). G1 flags 27 (it omits T**s Ass Drug Money). S1 flags 27 (it omits Personal Best). See the conflicts section.
- **The 100% checklist is hidden until the story ends.** It then appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Progress made during the story already counts, and nothing on it is missable (P1, P2, B2, S1, S2): **high**. PowerPyx and GTABase both put it at about 60% of true completion.
- **Time:** PowerPyx says about 200 h to Platinum (story 60–80 h, plus about 100 h of checklist, plus about 10 h of cleanup). Push Square says 250+ h (its body text says "up to 300 hours or more"). GTABase's roadmap says about 200 h, but its 100% page says 100–120 h for 100% including the story. Difficulty is rated 4/10 (P1, S1).
- Manual saves: 15 slots + 1 autosave. PowerPyx advises keeping a save at the start and end of each chapter, because there is no mission replay (P1, S1).

## Completionist "do this before X" (whole game)

1. **Chapter 1, before the mission Tourist Trap (the Ch1 point of no return):**
   - **Meditate with Lucia** when she offers on the balcony (one morning after Down to Earth). It is gone after Ch1, and it may be needed for Positive Mental Attitude. Worst case, per P5: replay from Ch2 (P1, P5, B1, R1, R2). **High.**
   - Read the 5 magazines in Jason's house (Ashes of Glory, Lethal By Design, Orca, Off the Hook, Body Copy). These copies are lost after Ch1, though PowerPyx says the titles appear elsewhere too (P6, R2).
   - Starting Tourist Trap locks you into 3 missions into Ch2. You lose the Ch1 safehouse, wallet money, stash, BuckMe savings (only up to $5,000 of investments survive) and personal vehicles (including Ultimate Edition ones). Jason's fitness is temporarily reset. Unique weapons and Lucia's relationship level carry over (G2). R2 advises spending money, collecting Donnie's lost drugs and finishing Shirley's quests first. Not trophy-critical.
   - Unique weapon Donnie's H&L .38 can only be taken from his car's trunk during Don Gone (G2). It is not in the 100% checklist (only standard-issue weapons count).
   - Donnie's drop-off (better narcotics pay) ends after Swamped (G2). Money only.
2. **Kyle / McKittrick Farm (Strangers & Freaks):** if Kyle dies or the plane crashes during Check Out McKittrick Farm, the strand can soft-lock. Game8 says the workaround of returning to the farm and stealing the plane has "been fixed", and that Kyle then texts the next mission. Keep a separate save (G2). G2 also claims this blocks 100%, but PowerPyx and GTABase say Strangers & Freaks isn't needed for 100%. Only the trophy is at risk (there are other strands, but the full qualifying list is unconfirmed). **Medium.**
3. **Chapter 2:** Starlet Motel magazine copies (Help Me Help You Help Me, Wizard Wang) are gone after Ch2 (P6). The home video-game TV is unavailable for part of Ch2 but returns in Ch3 (P2). Kenton's dive sites pause in Ch2 and return in Ch3 (G4).
4. **Chapter 3:** the Bayside apartment magazine copies (Passion B.C., Two Pricks in the Bush) are gone after Ch3 (P6).
5. **Chapter 4, first mission (Ocean Beach):** before leaving Boobie's apartment, solve the laptop puzzle to get a free garage and a free Standout car (Vanessa's Growler). Steal Randy's Wish List cars from the Y Hotel driveway before it becomes your safehouse (G2). Not trophy-critical.
6. **Anytime:** avoid the **Irredeemable** criminal profile. It is permanent and makes police more aggressive, but it does not block trophies (P1, B1). Lucia and Jason's relationship status doesn't affect trophies (P1). Many black/pink optional missions expire, and time-limited "hourglass" quests fail. Neither matters for trophies, given 200–300+ optional tasks (P1, P2). Some fences disappear permanently but have no quests (P1).
7. **After the story:** all trophy quest-givers (Kenton, Andy, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) remain in free roam. There is no mission replay, so keep chapter-boundary saves (P1, B1).
