# GTA VI: optional side content available by the end of Chapter 2 (RESTRICTED: research only, contains spoilers)

- **Date checked: 2026-11-22** (launch +3 days). Several of the pages below were published or updated *today* (the PowerPyx pages for The Final Flush, Retest Freddie's Defenses and The Unknown Stuntman; the Push Square guide for Wyman's cars; the GTABase random-event map). Guides are still work in progress.
- **Scope:** optional content a player can do **before starting "Checking Out"** (the Chapter 2 finale), in Port Gellhorn / Kelly County, the Leonida Keys / Grassrivers and nearby early areas, that the app does **not** already cover. Excluded as already covered: Check Out McKittrick Farm, Pick Up a Gun, Impounded Vehicle, Thrillbillies, Twist Wrist Smash Grab, Hit Avery Pawn, Hit Kingpin Bowling, Veteran Hunted, Collect Chemicals for Kyle, Buddy?, Moses requests and Moses' gift, Dunk fencing, store robberies, robbing criminals (gang operations), Family Savings, garages, pastimes. Also covered elsewhere in the app, so only cross-referenced here: the UFO roadside event, Bigfoot sighting, jet-ski dog, yard-sale hat, ghost ship and gator (`try-this*.ts`), Kenton's dive sites and Bret's target decks (`completion.ts`).
- **Map access:** there are no hard region locks after Chapter 1, and Vice City is reachable by road during Chapter 2 (earlier passes: GamesRadar map page and Game8; see `CHAPTER2_EXCERPT_PASS.md` 8.3 and `VIRAL_STUNTS_2.md`). So "Any Chapter" content located in Vice City or Leonard County is technically doable in Chapter 2.
- **Method:** pages were fetched with `curl` into the scratchpad (`sidech12/raw/`, never executed) and converted to text with a `python3 -I` script. All wording here is my paraphrase. Where a host was blocked by the session's network policy, only search-engine snippets were seen, and those rows say "snippet".
- **Blocked this pass (not read directly):** gamerant.com, ign.com, thegamer.com, videogameschronicle.com, sportskeeda.com, dexerto.com, keengamer.com, beebom.com, alcasthq.com, culturedvultures.com, esports.gg, meowdb.com, gameta.gg, youtube.com. Facts from them are marked **snippet**.
- **Confidence key:** **high** = official, or 2+ independent reputable pages that I read agree. **medium** = one reputable page read, or PowerPyx + GTABase only (GTABase credits PowerPyx and often paraphrases it; count the pair as about 1.5). **low** = a user-submitted map entry, or snippets only.
- **GTABase random-event map caveat:** the 181 entries on `gtabase.com/gta-6/map/?feature=random-event` are largely **user-submitted** (some written in the first person, one in French). Region labels are coarse and sometimes wrong. Treat every row sourced only from it as **low**.

## 1. Sal: "Sal, Shooting Victim" and "Revenge on Kolt Associates" (the owner's "helped Sal and got his car")

- **What it is:** a **random-event chain**, not a listed Strangers & Freaks mission. No PowerPyx, Game8, Push Square or PC Gamer page mentions Sal. The PowerPyx sitemap (405 GTA 6 URLs) has no Sal page, and neither does the PowerPyx contacts or characters list.
- **GTABase random-event map (user-submitted, low):**
  - **#118 "Sal, Shooting Victim"** (Port Gellhorn): robbers shot Sal while trying to take his wallet. He needs a ride and help with something.
  - **#129 "Revenge on Kolt Associates"** (Port Gellhorn): Sal asks you to get revenge on **Booker** at **Kingpin Bowling** (a Kolt & Associates site). The entry's wording "Sal, who shot himself" is probably a contributor typo for "who was shot".
- **The reward car (GTABase vehicle DB, medium for existence):** **"Dundreary Sal's Chino"**, a variant of the Chino '65, muscle class, 2 seats, RWD, Standout set **Retro**. Its description reads like in-game text: a custom '65 Dundreary Chino owned by an elderly vigilante named Sal, given to you for helping in his war against the Kolts.
- **Snippets only (YouTube, low):** a video titled "Sal's CHINO" says Sal gave **Jason** his Chino after the quest.
- **Unlock:** **unknown.** It's a Port Gellhorn encounter, so it fits Chapter 2. Part 2 sends you after the Kolts at Kingpin Bowling. The bowling robbery itself opens after Coffee Break, but no source says the Sal chain needs it. The owner has finished it, so it is available ≤ end of Chapter 2.
- **Character, time, cash payout, missability, trophy credit:** all **unknown**. Cash is not stated anywhere I read, so don't show one. Practical note from the car page: Standout cars can be kept by garaging or registering them, so remind the player to park Sal's Chino in a garage or register it before Checking Out (that mission impounds personal cars; see `CHAPTER2_VERIFICATION.md`). This tip is inferred from the general rule, not from a Sal-specific source.
- **Conflicts:** none, since there is only one publisher.
- **Confidence: low–medium.** The existence of both events and the car is backed by two separate GTABase pages, a YouTube title and the owner's own report. Every other detail is unverified.

## 3. The Final Flush (Meet at Frank's Waffles): Dr. Merle / No Flushers

- **Type:** Strangers & Freaks, a one-mission strand. **Counts** for the S&F trophy (PPX-FLUSH, PPX-TR, PPX-SF "Any Chapter").
- **Unlock:** scan a **No Flusher poster/QR code** with WAiNK. They are usually near bathrooms, even ones you can't enter. PPX names one at the Watson Bay campground. GB-RE #36 puts a QR code in Hamlet. Snippets also mention a Corsair Key bathroom, the Nine1Nine strip club bathroom and a Port Gellhorn notice board. Then **reply "TRUTH"** to the text that follows, and the mission is marked. Snippet (outputlag): the marker never appears if you don't answer the text.
- **Area:** starts at Frank's Waffles, **Tequesta (Vice City)**. You drive Dr. Merle's van to the sewage treatment plant in **Gloriana Key**. Snippet: you meet him at night.
- **Who:** Lucia or Jason, solo.
- **Reward:** **permanent stamina increase.** No cash is mentioned. You wake up back at your current home (PPX). Snippets (GameRant, games.gg) agree on the stamina boost, and some say "max stamina".
- **Tips:** Defuse the worker who stops you at the gate. Inside, use the upper-walkway console (turn all the lights green, then pull the lever) to open the floor. Send Dr. Merle out, then get in the chamber yourself before security arrives.
- **Missable / soft-lock:** not stated as missable (PPX lists it as Any Chapter).
- **Not to confuse:** "No Flusher Tags" are a separate WAiNK collectible (utility markings), first flagged by a text from Cal (PS-NOFL, PPX-NOFL).
- **Confidence: medium–high** (PPX read in full, GTABase map entries #36/#83, and 4 snippet publishers agree).

## 4. Retest Freddie's Defenses: Freddie

- **Type:** Strangers & Freaks, a one-mission strand. **Counts** for the S&F trophy (PPX-FRED, PPX-TR).
- **Unlock (any chapter):** first **break into Freddie's compound on your own**, with no marker. On the east side, use the loader to push the lone container aside and reveal a staircase. Then cross the proximity mines and shoot the 2 explosive drones. Freddie praises you over the intercom. A few in-game days later he texts and the mission is marked.
- **Area:** north side of **Lake Leonida, North Leonard County** (PPX). GB-RE #156 "Paranoid Prepper's Bunker" describes the same place but labels it "Ambrosia".
- **Who:** either.
- **Mission:** the tunnel is now sealed. Climb the water tower on the NE corner, drop onto the building, then deal with the mines and two drone waves (2, then 3). Bring an automatic weapon. Freddie tours you round the bunker, and you can answer a caller on his podcast. When a hurricane starts you choose to leave or stay, and the mission completes either way.
- **Reward:** **$10,000** (PPX). Snippets (leonidainteractive, games.gg) say **$5,000 plus his "Dubster" vehicle**, and that staying is the paying option. **Conflict, unresolved.** Finishing it **unlocks hurricane weather** (PPX).
- **Confidence: medium** (one full page plus a user map entry and snippets; the payout conflicts).

## 5. Gellhorn's Missing Tourists (missing persons / serial killer)

- **Start:** look at the **missing posters on the wall of the Starlet Motel reception**. This adds "Gellhorn's Missing Tourists" to your map (GB-OVN tip). Snippets add that it gives you a Kelly County tip-line contact that never answers, and that there is no mission marker. Available from **Chapter 2**.
- **Leads (GB-RE, user entries, low):** #122 a pink car with plate **PRTYGRL** hidden under another car, body in the trunk; #127 a sunken car at the edge of a lake, with only the roof racks showing; #149 a poster under the floorboards of an RV with "no lives" sprayed by the door; #130 "Investigate the Photos". Snippets: an abandoned warehouse north of Moses' garage (crawl in through a hole on the right side); the three victims are at a junkyard east of the Pay 'n' Spray, a trailer park in north Port Gellhorn, and a submerged car in a small lake west of town near Wild Hog Point.
- **Ending (snippets, conflicting):** after all three are found, the tip line calls you to the warehouse. Guides disagree on whether going with your partner skips the kidnapping/mine sequence. The killer's name is spelled "Alvan West" or "Alvin West". The killer's glasses can be picked up by **Jason only**. **No cash reward.** One guide reports a bug where going as Jason forces a switch to Lucia.
- **Missable / 100% / trophy:** unknown. Not on PowerPyx, Game8 or GTABase mission lists.
- **Location conflict:** guides disagree on where the second victim is (trailer floor in north Port Gellhorn vs. near the water tower north of town).
- **Confidence: low–medium.** Only GTABase was read directly (one tip and user map entries). The rest are snippets from 6+ secondary sites that broadly agree.

## 6. Find Cars for Wyman / "Classic Car Collection" (Ultimate Edition only)

- **Edition gate:** **Ultimate Edition only.** Standard Edition owners don't get this questline (PPX-WYMAN, PS-WYMAN, PCG-UE, GRD-SIDE). **Only show it to UE owners**, or label it clearly.
- **Unlock:** after Thrillbillies, UE owners get the **'67 Vapid Dominator (Dominator Buggy)** marked with a purple icon. Collect it from the **Paradise Garage, Watson Bay** (PCG-UE: this also gives you that garage, with a weapon locker and stolen-goods storage). Wyman then texts, and "Find Cars for Wyman" appears under Map index > Exploration (PS-WYMAN, PCG-UE, PPX-WYMAN). All of this is Chapter 2.
- **How it works:** **WAiNK-scan** the scrap cars for each model. Wyman then calls asking for **$1,500** labor (pay through BuckMe payment requests), and you collect the car at his scrapyard. To keep it, register it at a Pay 'n' Spray (**$100**, PS) or garage it; otherwise it goes back to Wyman's workshop (PS).
