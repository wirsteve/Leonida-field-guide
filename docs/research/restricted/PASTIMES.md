# GTA VI pastimes and leisure activities (free roam)

Researched 2026-11-22. The game launched 2026-11-19. Every source below was checked on 2026-11-22.

**Player context assumed:** mid Chapter 2, based in Port Gellhorn (Kelly County), with most of the map reachable by road.

**Spoiler rule:** player-facing fields name no story missions and no characters the player may not have met. Story gates appear only in the **Internal note** lines.

**Confidence:** H = two or more independent post-launch sources agree, or one detailed hands-on guide is fully read. M = one post-launch source was read. L = search snippet only, or a source that mixes pre-launch and post-launch text.

Common facts used in several sections:

- **Character switching.** Playing as both Jason and Lucia, and calling your partner along, unlocks partway through Chapter 2. Sources: PPX-MED, PPX-TR.
- **Map filters.** The map has category filters. Most activities sit under **Map > Activities (dumbbell icon)** and appear as blue icons once you get within about 100 m (PPX-RACE, PPX-DRONE).
- **Activity progress** is tracked under **Progress > Activities** (PPX-100).
- **Focus.** Playing golf, pool, basketball, shooting ranges and fishing permanently extends the Focus bar (PPX-STATS, GR-STATS, PS-HOOPS).
- **Master of None trophy.** It needs one of each of 9 activity types: Fishing, Hunting, Golf, Shooting Ranges, Pool, Basketball, a Video Game, winning a Race / Race Challenge / Drone Race, and a Stunt (PPX-TR).

## 1. Mini golf

- **What it is:** Themed putting courses with obstacles, scored by strokes.
- **How it works:** Few controls are documented. Fewer strokes is better, and courses track best score and holes in one. One snippet says you buy a round at the counter (GameRant snippet on Frontier Adventure Golf, L).
- **Where:** There are 3 courses.
  - **NEAREST: Dino Golf Challenge**, Frogswater, Kelly County.
  - King Gator Adventure Golf, Buckskin Swamp (listed under Vice-Dale County).
  - Frontier Adventure Golf, Waning Sands, Leonard County.
  - Map filter path is not documented. Track progress under **Progress > Activities > Sports**, where each course has its own entry.
- **When:** No unlock gate is documented, and the courses appear to be open in free roam (M).
- **Cost:** Not stated. A Polish guide (ppe.pl, snippet only, not an allowed host) says Dino Golf is an abandoned course you can play free. **L, unverified.**
- **Characters:** Both. Nothing says it is limited to one character.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - **100% checklist: yes**, "2 of 3 Mini Golf Locations Played". The two full courses do not count (PPX-100, PS-100).
  - Sports challenges: "Golf Rookie" (18 holes at King Gator or Frontier in under 30 min, 3 under par or better), "Golf Pro" (both courses in one day at par or better) and "Double Hole In One" (King Gator or Frontier). Dino is not part of these challenges (PPX-CHAL).
  - Counts toward the Golf type for Master of None.
- **Confidence:** H for existence and locations (PPX-GOLF, PPX-100, PPX-CHAL, GB-GOLFMAP); L for cost and controls.

## 2. Golf (full courses)

- **What it is:** Golf on two regular courses, with "Golf Challenge" objectives such as playing 9 or 18 holes.
- **How it works:**
  - The game tracks completed challenges, best score and holes in one. Mount Hailes also has a driving-range record (GB-GOLFMAP).
  - Game8's review describes 9- and 18-hole objectives at specific courses (G8-REV).
  - Swing controls were not found in any allowed source.
- **Where:**
  - Leaf Links Golf Challenge, Leaf Links, Vice Beach (Vice City area).
  - Mount Hailes Golf Challenge, Mount Hailes Club, Waning Sands, Leonard County.
  - **Neither course is in Kelly County.** Both are a long drive from Port Gellhorn.
  - Track progress under Progress > Activities > Sports.
- **When:** No gate is documented (M).
- **Cost:** Not stated.
- **Characters:** Both. No restriction is documented.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - **100%: no.** The full courses do not count toward the mini-golf line (PPX-100).
  - Counts as the Golf type for Master of None.
  - Golf builds Focus (PPX-STATS).
- **Confidence:** H that the courses exist; M for tracking details. Hole count conflicts: GolfMagic (snippet) says both courses are 18 holes, ppe.pl (snippet) says Leaf Links has 9.

## 3. Pool (8-ball)

- **What it is:** 8-ball pool with physics. Rockstar's interview commentary describes it as using proper physics (GR-DARTS).
- **How it works:**
  - Take a cue from the wall to start.
  - Some tables are solo practice. Others have an NPC opponent you can challenge for a small fee or stake (GR-POOL).
  - Matches take a while, so don't start one while a timed quest (hourglass icon) is running.
- **Where:**
  - There are 17 tables in total, and 7 of them have an opponent (PPX-POOL).
  - **NEAREST:**
    - **Delights Cabaret, Port Gellhorn** (table in the centre of the building). This is an adult venue; see section 17.
    - **Kowaachobee Cafe & Bar, Buckskin Swamp** (Kelly County, just off the highway; table on the west side).
  - Others:
    - The Rusty Anchor, Key Lento. The opponent there is not always present.
    - Skeeter's, Hamlet.
    - Zotico's, Balsero, Vice City.
    - The Thirsty Wolf, Belville, Leonard County.
    - Jacob's Tavern, Milport, Lummox County.
  - Solo tables are common around Key Lento (GR-POOL).
  - Track progress under **Progress > Activities > Sports > Pool**.
- **When:** Available from early in the game (GR-POOL).
- **Cost:** "A small amount" to play an NPC (GR-POOL). No exact figure is given.
- **Characters:** Both. No restriction is documented.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - **100%: yes**, "5 of 7 Pool Opponents Defeated" (PPX-100, PS-100).
  - Challenges: "Pool Streak" (win at 3 different locations in a day without losing or quitting), "Triple Bank" (bank the cue ball off 3 rails before potting) and "8-Ball Break" (sink the 8-ball on the break) (PPX-CHAL).
  - Counts for Master of None, and builds Focus.
- **Confidence:** H.
- **Internal note:** PPX-POOL says that if you don't beat the Delights opponent before the end of Chapter 2, you can do it later "once it is open again". In other words, Delights closes temporarily at some point in the story. Do not show this to the player. Just recommend Delights now.

## 4. Basketball (hoops)

- **What it is:** Free-form shooting at hoops around the world. There are no full pickup games: Rockstar left out full matches in favour of free movement and shooting (GR-DARTS).
- **How it works:** Every hoop has a ball nearby. Pick it up with **L1**, aim with **L2** and throw with **R2**. Angle and distance change how hard the shot is (PS-HOOPS). Scoring once at a hoop marks it complete.
- **Where:**
  - There are 100 hoops in total.
  - **NEAREST:**
    - Port Gellhorn trailer park, just north of Sarena's Bargain Store.
    - Avery Allen & Co. Aviation Services, North Kelly County, east of Manifest Canyon.
    - Both from PS-HOOPS, which has only 11 hoops listed so far.
  - Map filter: **Map > Exploration (magnifying glass) > Points of Interest > Basketball Hoops**. Completed hoops stay marked.
  - Track progress under Progress > Activities > Basketball (PPX-100).
- **When:** From the start.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Characters:** Both.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - **100%: yes**, "90 of 100 Basketball Hoops". Each one must be a different hoop (PPX-100, PS-100).
  - Challenges: "Basketball Rookie" (3 different hoops in a day), "Basketball Streak" (10 in a row in under 2 minutes), "Basketball Tour" (a hoop in every county) and "Basketball Pro" (all 100 hoops) (PPX-CHAL).
  - Counts for Master of None, and builds Focus.
- **Confidence:** H.

## 5. Fishing

- **What it is:** Catch-and-release rod fishing from docks, shorelines and boats. There are 35 species, and you cannot keep the fish (GB-FISH, GRD-SIDE).
- **How it works:**
  1. Pick up a rod from a dock or boat. Rods start with a small saltwater lure.
  2. Use Focus (L3+R3) to show fishing spots, which appear as white patches on the water.
  3. Hold L2 to ready the rod, then hold and release R2 to cast. A longer hold casts further.
  4. Reel slowly with the right stick, then press R2 on a bite to hook the fish.
  5. Pull the left stick against the fish's direction to tire it, then rotate the right stick to reel it in. If it thrashes, stop reeling, or the line can snap.
  6. The reel input can be changed to tap or hold under Settings > Gameplay > Accessibility > Fishing.
  - Sources: G8-FISH, GB-FISH.
  - Use freshwater lures in rivers and lakes, and saltwater lures at the coast, docks and sea.
- **Where:**
  - Rods are on docks and boats across Leonida (G8-FISH).
  - GTABase maps 35 fishing hotspots.
  - **Kelly County spots are not individually named** in the sources read. The county has rivers and swamps, for example the Emerald River at Camp Moonflower and Buckskin Swamp, but these are not confirmed as fishing hotspots.
  - Lures are sold at Ammu-Nations whose map icon lists a fish under "Features", and at Chuck's Tackle & Shot in Key Lento.
  - Map filter path is not documented.
- **When:** From the start of free roam.
- **Cost:**
  - Small freshwater and small saltwater lures are owned from the start.
  - Medium freshwater lure $45, medium saltwater lure $56, large saltwater lure $125 (GB-FISH).
  - A special Candy Cane lure is found as a pickup southwest of Ambrosia in Kelly County (GB-FISH).
- **Characters:** Both. No restriction is documented.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - Trophy **"Baited My Tackle"**: beat a friend's fishing challenge. It is marked with a purple icon west of Key Lento (PPX-TR).
  - 100%: there is no fishing line, but those fishing challenges count toward "100 Optional Tasks" (PPX-100).
  - Survival challenges: "Big Fish" (a fish of 20 lb or more), "Catch of the Day" (3 fish with each lure type in a day), "Angler" (4 species in under 6 minutes) and "Pro Angler" (every species) (PPX-CHAL).
  - Studying fish (swimming near them works) counts toward "100 of 255 Animals studied".
  - Counts for Master of None, and builds Focus.
- **Confidence:** H.
- **Internal note:** The fishing challenge comes from Cal and unlocks after the Chapter 1 mission "Feng Shui (Meet Cal)" (PPX-TR). The questline is "Cal – Catch A Bigger Fish".

## 6. Hunting

- **What it is:** Hunting wild animals with firearms in rural areas. It mainly earns money.
- **How it works:**
  - A clean head or heart shot leaves the carcass "pristine" and pays more. Too many shots ruin it (PPX-100, PPX-TR).
  - Large animals are heavy, so a suitable vehicle helps with transport (Push Square hunting-deck snippet, L).
  - No butcher or pelt-crafting system is confirmed post-launch. GTABase and GamesRadar mention crafting, but that is pre-launch text (L).
- **Where:**
  - Wilderness and swamp areas, especially the countryside and swamps in the north (PPX-100).
  - Lummox County and Mount Kalaga National Park are cited most.
  - **Kelly County:** the swamps and countryside are named only in general terms.
  - Once unlocked, the hunting-request tracker is at **Map > Activities (dumbbell) > Target Deck**.
- **When:**
  - Shooting animals is possible in free roam.
  - A structured hunting-request questline (15 "Target Decks") opens partway through Chapter 2 and is **Jason only**.
- **Cost:** No licence fee is documented. The questline gives you a hunting rifle, and you can collect it from any Ammu-Nation weapon locker.
- **Characters:**
  - The hunting-request questline is **Jason only** (PPX-100, PPX-TR).
  - General hunting is not stated to be limited to one character.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - **100%: yes**, "15 of 15 [hunting] Target Decks" (PPX-100).
  - Trophy **"Fair Game"**: hunt a unique animal for the questline (PPX-TR).
  - Challenges: "Alligator Kills", "Hunting Spree" and "Legendary Hunter" (kill all 8 unique animals) (PPX-CHAL).
  - Counts for Master of None.
- **Confidence:** H for the questline; M for free-roam hunting.
- **Internal note:** The questline is Bret Vogel's. It is marked by text after Chapter 2 "Coffee Break", starts with "Veteran Hunted (Meet Bret)" in Lummox County, and the rifle is the Killtelligence SM-69. Do not name Bret to the player if they haven't met him yet.

## 7. Shooting ranges

- **What it is:** Scored or timed target challenges with Bronze, Silver and Gold medals.
- **How it works:**
  - Buy a waiver pass from the clerk. The challenges are then marked at the range.
  - **Rodgers Ranch** is stationary score-attack. Hitting the body before the head builds a multiplier, and a miss lowers it. Courses: Market, Zombie, Hunting, Saloon, Cops & Robbers and Alien Range, each with 4 difficulties.
  - **Griffin** is a run-and-gun obstacle course on a timer. A bullseye takes 1 s off. Courses: 3-Point, Hook, Loop, Maze, Compound and Villa Range, each with 3 difficulties.
  - Finishing or quitting any challenge permanently raises Focus. Market Range Beginner at Rodgers takes about 5 s and can be repeated to farm Focus.
  - Sources: PPX-RANGE, PPX-STATS.
- **Where:**
  - **NEAREST: Rodgers Ranch & Shooting Range, north-east edge of Port Gellhorn, Kelly County.**
  - Griffin Shooting Range, north-west Vice City.
  - Map filter path is not documented. Track progress under **Progress > Activities > Shooting Ranges**.
- **When:** Open in free roam. GR-STATS recommends Rodgers as the best way to build Focus, with no gate mentioned (M).
- **Cost:** **$5 waiver pass** per range, plus an optional **$10 weapon rental** per challenge if you don't own the right weapon type (PPX-RANGE).
- **Characters:** Both. PPX suggests Lucia for Griffin because she runs faster.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - **100%: yes**, "42 of 42 Shooting Range Gold Medals" (PPX-100, PS-100).
  - Trophy **"The Shootist"**: every gold medal.
  - Challenges: "Marksman" (a medal at both ranges in one day) and "Pro Marksman" (PPX-CHAL).
  - Counts for Master of None.
- **Confidence:** H.
- **Data note:** PPX-RANGE's own medal totals are inconsistent (it says "54" and "72" medals in places but 42 golds overall). Use 42 golds and 12 challenges.

## 8. Top Fun drone races (time trials)

- **What it is:** Fly a racing drone through checkpoint courses against a par time.
- **How it works:**
  - There are 3 trials per location.
  - Beat par on the first two to unlock the third. Finishing the third with any time is enough for 100%.
  - Tip: hold the left stick forward all the way and avoid crashing (PPX-DRONE).
- **Where:**
  - There are 14 locations and 42 trials in total.
  - **NEAREST:**
    - **Port Gellhorn hub** (#11): Manifest Canyon, par 1:28; Red Apple Orchard, par 1:12; Port Gellhorn, par 1:39.
    - **Ambrosia** (#10, Kelly County): Captain O'Malley Marina, Allied Crystal, Waste Transfer Services.
    - **Buckskin Swamp** (#4): Kowaachobee Village, Swampy Sal's Safari Park, Camp Moonflower.
  - Others: Hamlet, Watson Bay, Capri, Balsero, Downtown, Dalton Island (boat or aircraft only), Vice Beach, Waning Sands, Draper Island, Moreau Sawmill and Millport.
  - Map filter: **Map > Activities (dumbbell) > Top Fun Drones**. Track progress under Progress > Activities > Top Fun Drones.
- **When:** Open in free roam. No gate is documented (M).
- **Cost:** Not stated.
- **Characters:** Both. No restriction is documented.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - **100%: yes**, "42 of 42 Drone Time Trials Completed" (PPX-100, PS-100).
  - Trophy **"Top Fun"**: win a race at every location.
  - A win counts toward Master of None.
- **Confidence:** H.

## 9. Races (Unique Races)

- **What it is:** Organised races: circuit, dirt-track, oval, elimination, demolition and street series.
- **How it works:** Each venue has 2 or more races, and winning one unlocks the next. You pay an **entry fee plus a vehicle rental fee**, and winning more than covers it (PPX-RACE).
- **Where:**
  - There are 40 races in total.
  - **NEAREST:**
    - **Gellhorn International Raceway**, south of Port Gellhorn: 11 races. Start with "Gellhorn Raceway – Introduction", which unlocks the 5-race amateur series "Keep it PG", then further series.
    - **Ambrosia Raceway**, north Ambrosia, Kelly County: 2 demolition races.
    - **Thrillbilly Mud Club**, North Kelly County: 6 dirt-track and 2 oval races.
  - Others:
    - Boone Speedway, Hamlet: 2 elimination races.
    - "Animal Crackers" races, 8 across Lummox and Mariana Counties.
    - "Hit 'N Run VC" street races, 9 across Vice City.
  - Map filter: **Map > Activities (dumbbell) > Racing**. Track progress under Progress > Activities > Racing.
- **When:**
  - Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Boone and Animal Crackers: no gate is documented (M).
  - Mud Club races: introduced through an optional job that opens partway through Chapter 2.
  - Vice City street races: **later in the story**.
- **Cost:** An entry fee and a rental fee. Amounts are not stated.
- **Characters:** Both. No restriction is documented.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - **100%: yes**, "30 of 40 Unique Races Won" (PPX-100, PS-100).
  - Trophy **"Earn Cash, Not Kudos"**: win 20 different races.
  - Challenges: "Winning Streak" and "Rally Winner" (3 straight wins in a day at Mud Club, Gellhorn and Boone) (PPX-CHAL).
- **Confidence:** H.
- **Internal note:** The Mud Club opens with Billy's optional "Thrillbillies (Check Out the Mud Club)", marked after Chapter 2 "Coffee Break". The street races start after Chapter 3 "Dealer's Choice" (they are part of Yago's Strangers & Freaks).

- **When:** As for races. The Mud Club trials need the Mud Club opened.
- **Cost:** Not stated.
- **Characters:** Both.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - **100%: yes**, "10 of 13 Race Challenge Gold Medals" (PPX-100).
  - **Conflict:** PS-100 says "30 Race Challenge Gold Medals". There are only 13 challenges, so PS-100 is probably an error. Trust PPX.
- **Confidence:** H for the list; M for the requirement.

## 11. Video games: home console and arcade

- **What it is:** Four playable in-game video games.
  - Tenebrae Lux: a greyscale first-person puzzle game.
  - Street Trash III: a top-down roguelike zombie shooter. Survive 18 minutes, collect 6 gears and extract.
  - Time Jackers Rewind: a top-down cyberpunk shooter with a time-slow power.
  - Bang For Your Buck: available on arcade machines only.
  - Sources: G8-VG, G8-ST3, PPX-VG.
- **How it works:** Interact with the controller at your home TV and pick a game from the JoySystem console, or use an arcade machine.
- **Where:**
  - Arcade (Bang For Your Buck): machines across the world. **NEAREST: outside the Bargain World clothing store, Port Gellhorn** (PPX-VG). There are also machines at the Wildland Zoo (G8-ZOO).
  - Home console: at the home TV.
  - Track progress under Progress > Activities > Games.
- **When:**
  - **The home console is temporarily unavailable during Chapter 2.** It is available in Chapter 1, comes back later in the story, and stays available after the story (PPX-VG, PPX-100).
  - **The arcade game is playable now.**
- **Cost:** Not stated.
- **Characters:** Both. No restriction is documented.
- **Trophy / 100%:**
  - **100%: yes**, "4 of 4 Video Games Played". Starting a game and exiting counts (PPX-100, PS-100).
  - Trophy **"Stick Fiddler"**: finish every level of any one game.
  - Counts for Master of None.
- **Confidence:** H.
- **Internal note:** The home console is at Jason's Key Lento house. Two of the games are introduced in Chapter 1 "Feng Shui". The console returns "from Chapter 3 onward" (PPX-VG).

## 12. Gym and workouts

- **What it is:** Strength and cardio workouts. They raise max Health (strength) and Stamina (cardio) and visibly change your body shape.
- **How it works:**
  - Buy a membership at the front desk.
  - Finish the day's workout (about 3 exercises) to lock in the gains, or they are lost.
  - Strength reps: hold L2+R2, ease off and squeeze again. Cardio punching bag: match the button press to the pink dial and score 90 or more (GR-STATS).
  - Each stat has 4 levels, and each level takes 3–4 full workouts (G8-FIT). Missing your workout window too often can drop you a level (GB-GYM).
  - Protein afterwards speeds recovery. Working out alongside your partner also completes their workout.
  - The Fitness phone app tracks your progress.

## Conflicts and data issues to flag

1. **Race Challenge 100% requirement.** PowerPyx says 10 of 13, and Push Square says "30". PowerPyx is likely correct, since only 13 challenges exist.
2. **Buckskin Swamp's county.** PowerPyx files the meditation and pool entries under Kelly County, but King Gator mini golf under Vice-Dale County. It is probably on the county border. Treat Buckskin Swamp as "near Kelly County".
3. **Strip-club prices.** PC Gamer gives $40 for a Delights private dance, and Game8 gives $300 (Vice City pricing).
4. **Leaf Links hole count.** Sources say 9 or 18. Both are snippets only.
5. **Shooting-range medal arithmetic.** PowerPyx's page is internally inconsistent. Use 42 golds and 12 challenges.
6. **GameRant blocked plain curl** with an HTTP/2 error. A browser user-agent worked for three articles. GameRant claims marked "snippet" were not read in full.
