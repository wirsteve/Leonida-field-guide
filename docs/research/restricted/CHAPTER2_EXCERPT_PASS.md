## (d) Canonical name mapping

### "Breakfast stickup" → **Breakfast Run**. Confidence: HIGH.
- Sources: game8.co/games/GTA-6/archives/639067 ; powerpyx.com/gta-6-breakfast-run-check-on-jason-walkthrough/ ; gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/breakfast-run (plus rarethief, cutscenewire and gta6info, all L).
- Evidence: a Chapter 2 story mission in which Jason (healed) and Lucia go out for breakfast. They rob **Hank's Waffles** diner in Port Gellhorn, then go on a stick-up spree through a liquor store, a thrift store, a gas station and another liquor store (the exact list of stores varies slightly by source). It ends with escaping the police. It unlocks the "Trust" trophy and gives personal vehicles. PowerPyx's objective-style subtitle is "(Check on Jason)". The informal "breakfast stickup" matches this mission's content exactly, and no other candidate turned up.
- **Important for the app:** Breakfast Run is around the **8th main mission of Chapter 2**, after Moses, Daily Driver and Slam Dunk. So the player is in **mid** Chapter 2, not right at the start. What comes next for them:
  - **Coffee Break** (main).
  - Lucia's car is **impounded** automatically (the Impounded Vehicle job).
  - The Coffee Break side jobs.
  - Afternoon Delight.
  - Prepare for Vice City ($15,000).
  - Checking Out, the point of no return.

### "Mo" → **Moses Watkins**, vehicle fence at Watkins Auto Parts, Port Gellhorn. Confidence: MEDIUM for identity, LOW for the nickname.
- Sources:
  - Identity and role: game8 Moses ; powerpyx Moses ; gtabase Moses ; gamerant how-to-sell-vehicles.
  - Surname and shop name: getleonida.com wiki, outputlag, gtaviceguides (L–M).
- Evidence:
  - Moses is the only documented early vehicle buyer. Lucia meets him in Chapter 2 through the mission "Moses", after Vicki sends her.
  - He buys untracked stolen cars and registers vehicles. The first registration is free.
  - Through Daily Driver and Steal Vehicles for Moses he gives the Port Gellhorn garage, and at the end a Sultan.
  - Other buyers (Pay 'n' Spray, and Randy in Vice City for tracked or high-end cars) are documented as coming later. So "a vehicle buyer the player has met by Breakfast Run" points clearly to Moses.
- The nickname "Mo" appears only on one low-quality fan wiki (getleonida.com). That same page says his profile states he **dislikes** being called Mo. No reputable or official source confirms it. Display the canonical name **"Moses"** and treat "Mo" only as the player's shorthand.
- Sanity check: the player has met Moses and has finished Breakfast Run, which comes after the Moses mission. This is consistent with the documented order.
