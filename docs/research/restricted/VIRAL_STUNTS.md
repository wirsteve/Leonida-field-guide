- Date checked: 2026-11-21 (all items)

Kotaku, GameSpot, Windows Central, Vice, Notebookcheck, Reddit, X and YouTube were not fetched (blocked or off the allow-list), so anything from them is a search-result snippet only and is labelled that way.

## 1. ATM Yank (Tow-Truck Withdrawal)
**What:** Back a tow truck (or a truck fitted with a winch) up to an outdoor ATM, hook it, and floor it to tear the machine out of the wall and drag it away.
**How:**
1. Get a tow truck. Police impound lots are the most reliable place to find one, and Game Rant names an impound lot in Port Gellhorn for Chapter 2. Once you can register a personal truck (Chapter 2), you can also add a winch to it at a Mod Shop.
2. Find an **outdoor** ATM. Indoor ones can't be pulled. The BuckMe phone app's ATM Finder will mark the nearest one.
3. Reverse until the back of the truck is flush with the ATM, lower the hook with the left stick until it latches, then drive off the other way.
4. Break it open with explosives, or keep dragging it around until it bursts.

**Needs:** tow truck or winch-equipped personal truck; explosives are optional.
**Where:** any outdoor ATM, e.g. around Port Gellhorn or on any city street.
**Risk:** police are alerted the moment it leaves the wall, so you get heat right away. Bank your existing wallet cash first, because pocket cash is lost if you're Busted or Wasted. Possible 100% overlap: PowerPyx says the "Robberies" category requires everything, and its examples are marked map targets (shops, jewellers, banks). No source says free-roam ATM pulls count, so treat it as unconfirmed. One fan wiki snippet links a separate *ATM Jobs* questline to Chapter 3, but that is the mission version; Game Rant describes free-roam towing as available now.
**Confidence:** high-ish. One reputable guide was read directly, and four more independent guides appeared in search snippets.

## 2. Secret Eyes Mode (Unofficial First-Person)
**What:** GTA VI has no first-person mode, but a camera trick players found on Reddit locks the game into a mostly working first-person view you can drive, run and shoot in.
**How:**
1. Settings > Controller: set the Touch Pad (PS5) or View button (Xbox) to the "Camera" function.
2. Sit in the driver's seat of any car and bring up your phone (D-pad Up).
3. Put the phone away and quickly tap the Touch Pad/View button. It can take a few tries.
4. To get back to third-person, save and reload. A fresh save works.

**Needs:** any car.
**Where:** anywhere.
**Risk:** this is a camera glitch, not a save exploit. No source reports save damage, but it may be patched. There are visual rough edges, such as shoulders clipping into view when you aim a long gun.
**Confidence:** high. Three reputable articles were read directly.

## 3. Lost at Sea? Call a Good Boy
**What:** Swim or drift far enough offshore and you'll hear barking, then a goggled "Cool Dog" pulls up on a jet ski and gives you a lift back to the beach, steering while you ride on the back.
**How:**
1. Go to a beach and swim, or drift on something small, well out into open water.
2. Wait out there until you hear barking.
3. Climb on behind the dog and enjoy the ride. Game8 says it may also introduce you to its friends and lead you to a small cash stash.

**Needs:** nothing.
**Where:** open ocean off a beach. Game8 names a specific bay, but a generic offshore spot seems to work.
**Risk:** basically none, apart from drowning or stamina if you go too far.
**Confidence:** high. Two reputable pages were read directly, and several outlets plus clips appeared in snippets.

## 4. Honk Rage and Drive-By Trash Talk
**What:** Lean on the horn or insult other drivers from your car. Some NPCs answer politely, some swear back, and some get out to fight.
**How:**
1. While driving, hold L3 to honk, and keep at it to really annoy someone.
2. Tap R3 (PS5) or R (Xbox) next to another car or pedestrians to trash-talk them. Jason or Lucia sometimes adds a middle finger.
3. On foot, hold L2/LT and press R1/RB to flip someone off directly.
4. Hold Up on the D-pad in a vehicle to get the full on-foot interaction menu while you sit behind the wheel.

**Needs:** any car (on foot works too).
**Where:** any busy street.
**Risk:** fistfights. Trash-talking **cops** gives you a one-star wanted level. Each driver has a limit on how many insults they'll respond to.
**Confidence:** high. Two reputable articles were read directly.
**Sources:**
- Game Rant, "How to Honk and Trash Talk While Driving in GTA 6" (2026-11-19): https://gamerant.com/gta-6-how-honk-flip-people-off/ (read)

## 5. Cat Roulette (Carjack With a Feline Passenger)
**What:** Steal a car that has a cat inside and the cat may jump on you mid-drive. The viral clip ends with Jason tossing the cat out, and the internet promptly named him the villain.
**How:**
1. Look for a parked or occupied car with a cat visible inside.
2. Steal it and start driving.
3. If the cat attacks, GameSpot (snippet) says a quick-time prompt stops it and you can pull over and let it out. Be the hero the clip wasn't.
4. Bonus wholesome version: find a bodega cat and give it chin scratches.

**Needs:** a car with a cat in it. These seem to be random, so you'll have to watch for one.
**Where:** any city street; the bodega cat is in a convenience store.
**Risk:** a normal car-theft wanted level.
**Confidence:** low by the rule. The reports are consistent across several outlets (GameSpot, Windows Central, Kotaku, Vice), but every one was a snippet.

## 6. Door-Prize Drive-By
**What:** You can whack pedestrians with your car door while driving, or kick them from a bike.
**How:**
1. Drive slowly alongside a pedestrian (or ride a bike).
2. Press the door/kick button that PowerPyx's tip shows. The glyph didn't come through in the text I read, so check the on-screen prompt.
3. Watch the ragdoll and the reactions.

**Needs:** a car or a bike.
**Where:** any sidewalk.
**Risk:** assault means a wanted level. Hitting cops escalates it, and lots of violence pushes your criminal profile toward "Psycho", which makes cops more trigger-happy.
**Confidence:** medium. One reputable guide was read directly, and I found no second source.

## 7. Indestructible Plane Pinball
**What:** Viral launch-day clips of jets bouncing off skyscrapers without exploding are real, but they come from a setting. Turn on "Reduced Vehicle Damage" and planes and cars survive impacts they normally wouldn't.
**How:**
1. Options > Settings > Gameplay, near the bottom: turn on **Reduced Vehicle Damage**.
2. Get a small plane from an airfield. PowerPyx lists aircraft locations, and aircraft respawn in the same spots.
3. Fly into a tower and watch it bounce, then turn the setting off again for normal play.

**Needs:** a plane (or try it with cars).
**Where:** any airfield, then the skyline.
**Risk:** stealing aircraft at an airport draws police. Push Square says the setting does **not** disable trophies or change the story, though the game warns that it changes the balance. Avoid the docked passenger jets at the international airport: stealing one is a trophy. Aircraft need fuel.
**Confidence:** high. Two reputable articles were read directly.
**Sources:**

## 8. The Literal Easter Egg (Tower Climb and Toss)
**What:** Climb a huge red-and-white cell tower for about five to eight minutes and you'll find a bird's nest of painted eggs at the top. Throw one off the edge to get a silly cutscene of where it lands.
**How:**
1. Find the giant red-and-white communications tower. Game Rant says it's "fairly early", two areas north of the opening region along the highway. Use their guide for the exact tower.
2. Hop the fence and climb, holding sprint to climb faster.
3. At the top platform, look right for the nest. Pick up an egg and throw it over the side.
4. Get down by parachute (base jump) or slide down the ladders, braking at the bottom of each one.

**Needs:** patience; a parachute helps.
**Where:** a tall tower south of the city (see the Game Rant guide).
**Risk:** falling is the main danger. Game8 says an influencer NPC is sometimes vlogging at the top, and if they're there you can't use your parachute.
**Confidence:** high. Two reputable guides were read directly.

## 9. Raccoon Mode (The Double-Shake Squat)
**What:** A Japanese player went viral after fumbling the many new controls and ending up as Jason crouch-walking around a dark parking lot while chugging two shakes he bought by accident. Recreate the meme.
**How:**
1. Go into a convenience store and buy two shakes or slushies.
2. Crouch and waddle outside, drinking.
3. Bonus: use "Rest" (hold Circle/B) at a pool edge or dock to sit and dip your feet, or out in the open to stand around awkwardly.

**Needs:** a few dollars.
**Where:** any convenience store or parking lot.
**Risk:** none.
**Confidence:** medium. One reputable article was read directly (the original post is on X, not fetched). The Rest controls come from a second PC Gamer article, also read.

## 10. Ghost Ship and Push-Start Shenanigans
**What:** Two little-known controls, both easy to turn into slapstick. You can push any vehicle by walking into it, and double-tapping exit leaves the engine running, which works on boats too: step out and stroll the deck while the boat keeps cruising forward.
**How:**
1. Walk into a car with L3 and hold L3 to shove it. Push a beached boat into the water, or roll a car somewhere it shouldn't go.
2. On a boat, get moving, then **double-tap** Triangle/Y to step away from the controls while it keeps going.
3. Walk around the deck as it cruises. Steer it away from anything solid first.

**Needs:** any car or boat.
**Where:** a marina or dock, or any street.
**Risk:** a runaway boat. Pushing someone else's car could annoy the owner.
**Confidence:** medium. One reputable article was read directly. PC Gamer's author says the boat behaviour is something they were "told" about, so the boat part is softer.
**Sources:**
- PC Gamer, "15 hidden GTA 6 controls and features" (2026-11-20): https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/15-hidden-gta-6-controls-and-features-you-should-know-about/ (read)

## 11. Don't Poke the Gator
**What:** Launch streamers went viral getting charged, bitten and dragged under by alligators after wading too close at swamp edges. Vice also says you can lure annoying NPCs near gators. Watch from a safe distance.
**How:**
1. Find a swampy lake edge where alligators bask.
2. Approach slowly and see how close you can get before it charges (or don't).
3. Have an exit route or a weapon ready.

**Needs:** nothing (a gun is advisable).
**Where:** swamps and lakes outside the city.
**Risk:** high chance of getting Wasted, which loses your pocket cash, so bank it first. Using NPCs as gator bait is assault plus wanted level, and sources disagree on whether "feeding" NPCs is a real mechanic (WikiGames says no).
**Confidence:** low. Only snippets and clips (Kotaku, Vice, IShowSpeed stream clips).
**Sources:**
- Kotaku animal roundup: https://kotaku.com/grand-theft-auto-6-gta6-cats-dogs-snake-pet-alligator-2000758120 (snippet only)
