## Endings (restricted)

Multiple endings are confirmed (G8-CHO cites Rockstar; G8-DIES; PCG-PC; GB-END), which is high confidence. **The 5-ending breakdown below comes from GTABase only** (GB-END plus the GB mission pages, all the same outlet). Parts are corroborated: Game8 confirms two endings where both die, one shot leaving the car and one a no-surrender charge; PCG confirms one ending needs $2M in Family Savings and that the final choices still matter. So the structure is **medium**, and the death endings and the $2M gate are high.

Choice points:
- **(A)** End of Protective Custody: Shoot Mendez, or wait about a minute for Take Deal.
- **(B)** The bridge in The End of the Road: Swim for It or No Surrender.

Inputs: the relationship level (4 tiers: Running on Empty, Not So Hot, Crazy in Love, Hot Together), Family Savings, and both characters' Criminal Profiles. Family Savings goals step up $5k → $15k → $100k → $2M; deposits can't be withdrawn (GB-END).

| # | Ending (GB naming) | How (GB) | Outcome (paraphrase) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mendez's Deal (bad) | Take Deal at (A) | They get Giorgio's files for Mendez and are cuffed; credits over swamp skulls. No bridge scene and no Cal post-credits scene |
| 2 | Failed Escape (bad) | Swim for It on a save that doesn't qualify (e.g. Psycho or Irredeemable profile) | Jason steps out still armed; both are shot dead |
| 3 | No Surrender (bad) | No Surrender at (B), whatever your stats | They ram the roadblock and are killed together |
| 4 | Jason Takes the Fall (good) | Swim for It + Hot Together + clean profile + under $2M | Lucia escapes into the water and Jason is jailed |
| 5 | Two Years Later (best) | Swim for It + Hot Together + $2M Family Savings + both profiles clean | Lucia pays to free Jason two years later and the family reunites |

- Story choices that feed the inputs (GB-END): sparing Arnold, the shark harpoon in Island Dreams, telling Lucia about Brian's retirement, Day Release interactions, refusing the director in Safe Word.
- Post-credits (bridge endings only): Cal is waiting on the hard-drive decryption (GB).
- There is also a hidden trophy, "Actions Speak Louder" (GB).
- A separate "secret ending" through the Tenebrae Lux mini-game appears in a GameRant search-result title only. **Not read: low.**

## After the story

- **Free roam continues.** After the credits you are put back just before the finale with both Jason and Lucia playable, even after endings where they die. The finale marker stays, so you can replay it for other endings (GB-END, GB-m). PCG-PC and PS-CH also say you return to the open world. This is high.
- **Cheats unlock.** GRD says finishing the story unlocks all cheats. GB-END says a Cheats tab opens with 61 codes: 4 usable at once, the rest unlocked by challenges. The unlock itself is high; the count is medium.
- **The 100% checklist expands** after the finale, so the completion percentage drops (GB-END): medium.
- All non-missable Strangers & Freaks and trophy questlines stay available after the story (PPX-FULL), and so do skipped side missions, vault jobs and collectibles (GB-END): medium+.
- Endings 4 and 5 can be upgraded by topping up Family Savings in free roam and replaying the finale (GB-END): medium.
